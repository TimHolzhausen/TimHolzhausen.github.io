Printing, cutting, binding
  • 100 % / Actual size. Never “Fit to page”, no borderless printing.
  • Double-sided, flip on the long edge. The sheets sit in the middle of the page, so front and back line up. Without duplex, print the odd pages, turn the stack over and print the even pages.
  • Laser printer, black as K. The pen only sees carbon black. The Ncode is pure black, your design is stored without any black (K = 0), so even dark areas of the design do not hide the dots. A PostScript driver passes everything through most cleanly.
  • Keep the design light behind the writing area. Large, very dark areas use a lot of toner and make the paper wavy.
  • Cutting: Front side up. Cut all horizontal lines at the side marks first, from the bottom up, then the vertical lines at the top marks.
  • Making a pad: Stack the sheets flush, clamp them between two boards with the top edge sticking out 2 mm, and brush on padding glue (or thinned PVA glue) in two thin coats. A piece of cardboard at the bottom makes it sturdy.
