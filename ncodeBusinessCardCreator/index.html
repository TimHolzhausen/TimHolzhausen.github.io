Printing and cutting
  • 100 % / Actual size. Never “Fit to page”, no borderless printing.
  • Double-sided flipping on the long or short edge, both work: the layout is symmetric. Without duplex, print page 1, turn the paper over, then print page 2.
  • Laser printer, black as K. The pen only sees carbon black. A PostScript driver passes the dots through most cleanly.
  • Cutting, step 1: Front side up. Cut all horizontal lines at the side marks, working from the bottom up. That way the marks stay on the remaining piece until the last strip is off.
  • Step 2: Stack the strips with the top strip (the one with marks along its top edge) on top. Put the left paper edge flush against the guide, then cut vertically at the top marks.
  • Every sheet carries the same codes. If you print several sheets, each code appears more than once.
